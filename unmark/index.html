Remove watermarks & objects

Paint it. Gone.

Drop a photo, brush over a watermark, logo, date stamp or stray object, and AI fills the gap with what should be there. It runs on your device, so your photo never leaves it.

No photo handy?

Paint, don't select

Brush or box over the mark. Several spots at once is fine.

Fills, doesn't blur

The AI rebuilds textures, edges and patterns behind the mark.

Stays on your device

No upload, no account, no watermark of ours on the result.

The first run downloads the AI model (about 110 MB) once; your browser keeps it for next time.

Tip: cover the whole mark, including its soft edge. For text, one wide stroke over the word works better than tracing letters.

Privacy

Photos are processed in your browser. They are not uploaded to or stored by this site. The page sets no cookies, runs no ads and has no visitor tracking. The AI engine and model are saved in your browser cache so the page starts faster next time. The server that hosts the page may keep standard access logs, such as your IP address.

Terms of use

Use Unmark only on images you own, or that you have the right to edit. Do not use it to remove a copyright notice, credit or watermark from someone else’s work in order to use that work without permission. Results are made automatically, can be imperfect, and are provided as is, without warranty of any kind. The AI invents plausible content to fill the gap, so do not rely on edited images as evidence, identification or records. Check each result before you use it.
