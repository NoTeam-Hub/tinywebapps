Vertical video editor

Drop your clips. Make it a reel.

No timeline to learn first. Drop videos and photos, then cut, caption and score them with simple controls, all on your own device. Export an MP4 or WebM for Reels, Shorts and TikTok.

Cuts to the beat

Add a song and Upright finds the beats, then snaps your photo cuts to them.

Private by design

Everything runs in your browser. Your clips never leave your device and are never uploaded anywhere.

Looks on top

Eight filters, transitions, animated text, emoji, SRT captions, a logo, and export in four formats.

Nothing to download: Upright has no AI model to load, so it starts instantly. Export records in real time, so a 30-second reel takes 30 seconds. Chrome and Edge work best.

Privacy

Clips, photos, music and logos are processed in your browser. They are not uploaded to or stored by this site. The page sets no cookies, runs no ads and has no visitor tracking. The server that hosts the page may keep standard access logs, such as your IP address.

Terms of use

You are responsible for having the right to edit and use every clip, photo, song and logo you process. Results are made in your browser, can be imperfect, and are provided as is, without warranty of any kind. Check each export before you publish it. Copyrighted music may be muted or removed by the platforms you post to.

Credits

Upright is built on standard browser features (Canvas, Web Audio and MediaRecorder) and uses no third-party code or models. Typefaces are Fraunces, Instrument Sans and JetBrains Mono (SIL Open Font License). The tracks in the Music library are original music made for Upright and are free to use in videos you make with it.
