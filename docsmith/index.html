Docsmith
Ready on your device
PDF & image toolkit

Type what you want. Docsmith does it.

Merge, split, rotate, delete, compress and convert PDFs and images by typing plain instructions, like “delete pages 2-4” or “compress to 500 KB”. It all runs on your device, and nothing is uploaded.

Or start from a task:

Type an instruction, or tap an example.

Working…

No pages yet. Add a PDF or some images.

Select pages by tapping them, then say “delete selected” or “rotate selected left”. Drag pages to reorder them, or use the arrows.

No images yet. Add some, then say “convert to png” or “compress to 100 KB”.

Just type it

Say “delete pages 2-4”, “rotate all right” or “move page 3 to the start”. No menus to hunt through. Tap or drag pages if you prefer.

Hit a file size

Compress a PDF or an image to about 500 KB or 100 KB, handy for forms with upload limits.

Many formats

Merge and split PDFs, make a PDF from photos, turn pages into JPG, PNG or WebP, and batch convert images.

Private by design

Your files are processed in your browser and never uploaded. No sign-up, no watermark.

Docsmith reads a fixed set of plain phrases. It is not an AI chatbot, which is why nothing has to leave your device. Password-protected PDFs can not be opened.

Privacy

PDFs and images are processed in your browser. They are not uploaded to or stored by this site. The page sets no cookies, runs no ads and has no visitor tracking, and it keeps nothing about your files after you close the tab. The server that hosts the page may keep standard access logs, such as your IP address.

Terms of use

You are responsible for having the right to use every document and image you process. Results are made automatically in your browser, can be imperfect, and are provided as is, without warranty of any kind. The command bar understands a fixed set of plain phrases and is not an AI assistant. Compressed PDFs turn pages into images, so their text can no longer be selected or searched. Check each result before you send it. Password-protected PDFs can not be opened.

Credits

PDFs are edited with pdf-lib (MIT), shown and rendered with PDF.js by Mozilla (Apache-2.0), and zipped with fflate (MIT). Typefaces are Fraunces, Instrument Sans and JetBrains Mono (SIL Open Font License).
